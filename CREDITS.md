## Fonts

The interface (HUD and menus) uses **Barlow** and **Barlow Condensed** (© 2017 The Barlow Project Authors,
https://github.com/jpt/barlow), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full license texts are next to the
files in `Content/Data/Fonts/OFL-Barlow.txt` and `Content/Data/Fonts/OFL-BarlowCondensed.txt`. Weights used: Barlow
Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold and Barlow Condensed Medium, SemiBold, Bold (TTF files unchanged).

**Rajdhani** (© 2014 Indian Type Foundry) for the HUD's and menus' text and numbers (Medium, SemiBold, Bold) and
**Cinzel** (© 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors, https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel) for titles (Bold, Black), both
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; full texts in `Content/Data/Fonts/OFL-Rajdhani.txt` and
`Content/Data/Fonts/OFL-Cinzel.txt`. TTF files unchanged (Rajdhani from google/fonts, Cinzel from NDISCOVER/Cinzel).

## Sound and music

- The hit, critical, kill, "not ready" and "ultimate ready" sounds are synthesised in code (`Source/ParagonArena/Arena/ArenaHitSound.h`).
- The heroes' voice lines are Epic's, from the Paragon packs.
- The teasers' music is synthesised from scratch in Python/numpy by `Tools/Teaser/music.py`, `music_v3.py` and
  `music_short.py` (no samples, no third-party audio).

## The project's own art

- Rock skirts generated by `Tools/Blender/rock_skirts.py` in Blender 5.2.
- The teasers' 3D title cards rendered by `Tools/Teaser/titles.py` in Blender 5.2 and edited by the `Tools/Teaser/edit*.py` scripts.
- Materials, hero portraits and ability icons for the HUD are made by the project's scripts and the in-game icon studio.
